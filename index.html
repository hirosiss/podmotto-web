Podmotto brings discovering, listening to, and understanding the world's podcasts into one app.

AI translated audio · Premium

Hear any episode in your own language.

Even if the show you want is in another language, Podmotto transcribes it, translates it, and reads it aloud in a natural voice — so you can follow it in your language, even without looking at the screen.

  • Audio is generated in chunks, so even long episodes start quickly.
  • Adjustable playback speed, with skip forward and back.
  • Resilient on weak connections, and auto-resumes if it stalls.

For creators

Podmotto isn't only for listeners. When your show is on Podmotto, people everywhere can follow it in their own language — so your ideas travel far beyond the borders of the language you record in.

  • Reach listeners who don't share your language.
  • Grow an audience across countries and regions.
  • No extra work — listeners choose the language on their side.

Private by design, fair to creators

We built Podmotto to respect both your privacy and the people who make the podcasts you love.

  • On your device, not our servers. Your transcripts and translations are stored only on your device — never saved to our servers and never shared with other users.
  • Your premium audio is yours alone. AI voices are generated uniquely for you, and are never reused or shared between users.
  • Creators stay in control. A show reaches more listeners only with its creator's consent — reach grows by opt-in, never behind their back.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are supported?

English, Japanese, Spanish, French, Korean, Chinese, German, Portuguese, and more. Whatever the original language, Podmotto translates and reads it in the language you choose.

Is translated audio free?

AI translated audio is a Premium feature. During the current TestFlight beta, Premium is free to try.

Can I bring my existing subscriptions?

Yes. Import from Spotify with one login, or use the browser tool for Apple Podcasts. RSS and OPML import are also supported.

What devices are supported?

iPhone is supported, and it works on iPad too.
